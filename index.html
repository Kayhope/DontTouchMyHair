Discover Our Hair Oil

Our specially formulated hair oil promotes hair growth, strengthens strands, and enhances shine. Crafted with high-quality ingredients, it's designed to make your hair flourish and become an essential part of your routine.

Ms. Tamlin K, Co-Founder and Representative of DON'T TOUCH MY HAIR, faced a recurring challenge with hair breakage in 2021. Despite her hair growing for several months, it would inevitably break off during the following months, creating a frustrating cycle that lasted for a year. Uncertain of the cause of this issue, Tamlin sought a solution in 2022, when her mother introduced her to home remedies that could potentially address her hair concerns. To her surprise, these remedies worked exceptionally well, offering significant improvement in the health and strength of her hair.

Amazed by the remarkable results, Tamlin recognized the potential for this natural remedy to benefit others facing similar hair challenges. Motivated by her success, she decided to further educate herself by consulting with professionals in the field to gain insight into which products and ingredients would be both safe and effective for hair care. With this newfound knowledge, she began crafting and selling her own hair oil, which has since helped many others achieve healthier, stronger hair. The positive feedback from satisfied customers continues to affirm the impact and success of her product.
